관세 발생 시 귀사 부담입니다.
attached shipped header days covered
2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
📦 Scene 1. Confirming Sample Details (샘플 발송 전 확인 통화)
David
Hi Sarah, calling to confirm the sample shipment details before we send.
Buyer
Hi David. Thanks for double-checking.
David
We're sending 100 sample sets via DHL Express. Should arrive in 3 to 5 business days.
Buyer
Perfect. What about customs duties?
David
Customs duties, if any, will be on your side. Most samples under $200 in value clear without duty.
Buyer
Good. And the shipping address?
David
I have your Boston office address on file. Should I send to a warehouse instead?
Buyer
Office is fine. We'll distribute internally.
✍️ Scene 2. Email Best Practices (이메일 작성 베스트 프랙티스)
Buyer
By the way, can you make sure the email includes the commercial invoice and packing list?
David
Of course. I always attach them together with the tracking number in the subject line.
Buyer
Tracking number in the subject line? That's unusual.
David
It makes it easy to search later if anything goes wrong with shipping.
Buyer
Smart. I'll start doing that too.
David
Also, I'll mention the estimated delivery and customs information upfront, before the attachments.
Buyer
Why upfront?
David
Most people skim emails on their phone. Critical info should be visible without scrolling.
Buyer
That's a great principle. The email should arrive in about an hour, right?
David
Yes, I'll send it right after this call.

MISSION
샘플 발송 안내 이메일 정보 전달형 구조로 작성하기
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 발송 완료와 트래킹 번호 먼저 알리기
② 예상 도착일 명시하기
③ 관세 부담 주체 명확히 안내하기
④ 첨부파일 (포장 명세서·상업 송장) 안내하기
