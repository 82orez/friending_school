PART 3 · Negotiation & Email · Unit 16
바이어 Sarah는 "6,000개는 부담스럽다. MOQ를 낮춰달라"고 요청한다. 민성(David)은 회사 정책과 바이어 요구 사이에서 협상해야 한다. "트라이얼 오더로 일단 진행하고 단계적으로 늘리는 방안"을 제안하며 윈윈 해법을 찾는다.

2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🤝 Scene 1. The MOQ Request (MOQ 인하 요청)
Buyer
I've been thinking about the order, and 6,000 units feels like a big commitment for a first batch.
David
I understand your concern about committing to a large first order.
Buyer
Could you meet us halfway on the MOQ?
David
What number did you have in mind?
Buyer
Honestly, 3,000 units would be much easier for us.
David
3,000 is below our standard MOQ, but let me see what I can do on my end.
Buyer
I appreciate that. We'd like to test the product before committing more.
David
Fair point. Let me think about how we can structure this.
💡 Scene 2. Finding a Win-Win (윈윈 솔루션 찾기)
David
Here's what I can propose — a trial order at 3,000 units for the cica cream only.
Buyer
Only cica? Why?
David
Cica is our top seller, with the highest market acceptance. It gives you the best chance for a successful trial.
Buyer
That makes sense. What about the pricing?
David
We'd waive the volume discount for the trial, but the unit price stays the same as our regular rate.
Buyer
No premium for the smaller quantity?
David
No premium. But we'd ask for a commitment — if cica sells well, the next order should be at least 8,000 units.
Buyer
That's reasonable. We can ramp up gradually as sales perform.
David
Exactly. This way, we both share the risk and the upside.
Buyer
I like this approach. Let's do it.

MISSION
MOQ 인하 요청에 윈윈 솔루션 제안하기
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 바이어의 우려를 먼저 인정하기
② 트라이얼 오더 옵션 제안하기
③ 베스트셀러 1개 SKU로 한정하는 이유 설명하기
④ 다음 오더 최소 수량 약속 받고 마무리하기
📝 EXAMPLE I understand your concern about committing to a large first order. Here's what I can propose — a trial order at 3,000 units for the cica cream only. Cica is our top seller, so it gives you the best chance for a successful trial. We'd waive the volume discount, but the unit price stays the same. The only condition is that if cica sells well, the next order should be at least 8,000 units. This way, we both share the risk and the upside.
