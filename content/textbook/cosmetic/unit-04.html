2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
✨ Scene 1. Introducing the Hero Product (시그니처 제품 소개)
David
Now I'd like to introduce our hero product — Myeongpum Gounbal foot cream.
Buyer
Myeongpum Gounbal? What does that mean in English?
David
It means 'premium beautiful feet.' It's been Korea's number one foot cream for five years in a row.
Buyer
Wow, that's a strong track record. What makes it special?
David
The key ingredient is 10% urea, which gently softens hard, cracked skin.
Buyer
Urea? That's a common ingredient. What's different about yours?
David
Our formula combines urea with shea butter and glycerin. It softens without irritation.
Buyer
Interesting. How quickly do customers see results?
David
Most users see visible improvement within two weeks of daily use.
💎 Scene 2. Texture & Customer Feedback (텍스처와 고객 후기 공유)
Buyer
Could I try a sample? I'd like to feel the texture.
David
Of course. Here, please apply a small amount to your hand.
Buyer
Hmm, it's rich but absorbs quickly. No greasy feeling at all.
David
Exactly. That's what our customers love most. The scent is also subtle and clean.
Buyer
I noticed. Very pleasant. What do online reviews look like?
David
On our official site, it has a 4.8 out of 5 rating from over 30,000 reviews.
Buyer
That's an excellent rating. I think this could work well in our stores.
David
Wonderful. Let me share the full product spec sheet by email.
