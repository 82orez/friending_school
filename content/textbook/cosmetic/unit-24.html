2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🎉 Scene 1. The Signing Moment (계약 체결의 순간)
David
Sarah, it's official. We just signed the agreement.
Buyer
Congratulations, David! And to your whole team.
David
Congratulations to you too. It's been an absolute pleasure working with you these past few weeks.
Buyer
Same here. You've made this process really smooth.
David
Thank you. I'm thrilled. This is just the beginning of a long partnership.
Buyer
I believe so too. When does the first shipment go out?
David
June 30, as planned. I'll send you a formal confirmation email today with all the milestones.
Buyer
Perfect. Let's celebrate tonight. Dinner on me this time.
🤝 Scene 2. Looking Ahead (장기 파트너십 비전)
Buyer
What's the next step after the first shipment?
David
I'd like to set up a quarterly review to align on strategy.
Buyer
Every three months? That works well for me.
David
First call would be 60 days after launch — we'll look at early sales data and customer feedback.
Buyer
And then we plan year two together?
David
Exactly. If California, New York, and Texas perform well, we expand nationally.
Buyer
I want to be in 25 states by year three.
David
Ambitious — I love it. Let's make it happen.
Buyer
By the way, when are you visiting Boston?
David
I'd love to come for your launch event. Let me know the date and I'll be there.
Buyer
Done. This is the start of something great.

MISSION
최종 계약 확인 이메일 작성하기 — 대미 장식
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 함께 일한 시간에 대한 감사 표현하기
② 공식 계약 체결과 첫 발주 일정 확인하기
③ '시작에 불과하다' 장기 파트너십 비전 강조하기
④ 분기별 전략 점검 등 향후 일정 제안하기
📝 EXAMPLE It's been an absolute pleasure working with you these past few weeks. I'm thrilled to confirm that we've officially signed the agreement. The first order will ship on June 30 as planned, and I'll personally oversee the quality checks before dispatch. This is just the beginning of a long partnership. Let's plan a quarterly review to align on strategy — first call 60 days after launch. If California, New York, and Texas perform well, we expand nationally. Let's make it happen together.
