2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🛑 Scene 1. The Hard Request (강한 요구)
Buyer
I reviewed your quote. To be honest, our team feels the unit price is too high.
David
I appreciate your feedback. What's your team looking for?
Buyer
We'd need at least a 20% discount to make the numbers work.
David
20% is quite significant. May I ask what's driving that target?
Buyer
Our retail margin requirements, plus marketing budget.
David
I understand the pressure. But I'd love to help, but a 20% discount isn't possible.
Buyer
Why not?
David
Our margins on this line are already quite tight. A 20% cut would mean selling below cost.
💡 Scene 2. Offering Alternatives (대안 제시)
David
Instead of a price cut, may I offer some alternatives?
Buyer
Sure, I'm open to ideas.
David
First, we could include 500 free samples for in-store promotion.
Buyer
Free samples — that helps with marketing costs.
David
Second, we could provide co-marketing support — high-resolution images, social media content kits, and English-language sales training.
Buyer
That's actually valuable. We've been struggling with content for Korean brands.
David
Third, if we hit certain sales milestones together, we can revisit pricing for the second-year contract.
Buyer
So a long-term pricing benefit tied to performance.
David
Exactly. We're looking for a win-win partnership, not a one-time deal.
Buyer
I appreciate this approach. Let me bring it back to my team.

MISSION
무리한 할인 요청을 정중히 거절하고 대안 제시하기
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 요구의 배경을 먼저 이해하려는 자세 보이기
② 정중하게 가격 인하 거절 이유 설명하기
③ 무상 샘플·마케팅 지원 등 대안 제시하기
④ 장기 파트너십과 단계적 가격 혜택으로 마무리하기
📝 EXAMPLE I appreciate your feedback. I'd love to help, but a 20% discount isn't possible — our margins on this line are already quite tight. Instead of a price cut, may I offer some alternatives? First, 500 free samples for in-store promotion. Second, co-marketing support including content kits and sales training. Third, if we hit sales milestones together, we can revisit pricing for the second-year contract. We're looking for a win-win partnership, not a one-time deal.
