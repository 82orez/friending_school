2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🔑 Scene 1. The Exclusivity Request (독점권 요청)
Buyer
I want to take this discussion one step further. We'd like exclusive U.S. distribution rights.
David
That's a significant request. Could you tell me more about why?
Buyer
We're willing to invest heavily in marketing, and we need to protect that investment.
David
I understand. We're open to discussing exclusive rights with the right partner.
Buyer
Good. So you'd consider exclusivity?
David
Yes, but we need to define the scope carefully. Exclusivity usually comes with a minimum commitment.
Buyer
What kind of commitment?
David
Typically an annual minimum sales target. Without that, exclusivity could limit our growth.
🗺️ Scene 2. Defining Scope (범위와 조건 정의)
David
Also, could we limit the territory to specific states first?
Buyer
What did you have in mind?
David
How about exclusivity in California, New York, and Texas for year one — then expand based on performance?
Buyer
That's a fair approach. What's the minimum sales target you're thinking?
David
For three-state exclusivity, we'd suggest an annual minimum of $1.5 million wholesale.
Buyer
$1.5 million is achievable with strong marketing. What if we don't hit it?
David
If you miss by more than 20%, exclusivity converts to non-exclusive automatically. No penalty, just opens the territory.
Buyer
That seems fair. What about the second-year terms?
David
If you exceed the minimum, we expand exclusivity nationally with a new target — say $4 million for year two.
Buyer
I like the structure. Exclusivity should benefit both parties, not just one.
David
Exactly. Let me prepare a draft term sheet for legal review.

MISSION
독점 판매권 협상 핵심 4가지 다루기
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 독점권 요청에 대한 열린 자세 표현하기
② 영역 범위 (특정 주) 제안하기
③ 연간 최소 매출 목표 명시하기
④ 목표 미달 시 자동 비독점 전환 조항 설명하기
📝 EXAMPLE We're open to discussing exclusive rights with the right partner. Exclusivity usually comes with a minimum commitment, so we need to define the scope carefully. Could we limit the territory to California, New York, and Texas for year one — then expand based on performance? For three-state exclusivity, we'd suggest an annual minimum of $1.5 million wholesale. If you miss by more than 20%, exclusivity converts to non-exclusive automatically. Exclusivity should benefit both parties, not just one.
