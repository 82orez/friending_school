2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
💄 Scene 1. The Origins of K-Beauty (K-뷰티의 시작)
Buyer
How did K-beauty become so famous worldwide? I'm really curious about its story.
David
It's gone through several major waves. The journey began in the 1990s.
Buyer
What happened in the 1990s?
David
Traditional Korean herbal medicine inspired our early cosmetics. Ginseng, green tea, snail mucin — all became hero ingredients.
Buyer
Snail mucin? That always surprises my customers.
David
It's known for its healing properties. Korean brands turned it into a beauty trend.
Buyer
What was the next big wave?
David
BB cream in the 2000s. It started as a doctor's product in Germany, but Korea reinvented it for everyday use.
🌟 Scene 2. The Global Boom (글로벌 확산의 시작)
David
After BB cream, sheet masks and 10-step skincare routines went viral around 2014.
Buyer
Yes, I remember when sheet masks were everywhere in U.S. drugstores.
David
Then came the K-pop and K-drama boom in the 2020s. That changed everything.
Buyer
How did that affect cosmetics?
David
Fans wanted to look like their favorite idols. Suddenly, every K-beauty product had a global market.
Buyer
That's a huge cultural advantage. Most other countries don't have that.
David
Exactly. Korean culture and beauty became inseparable. Now we're one of the leading cosmetic exporters worldwide.
Buyer
Where does your company fit into this story?
David
We've been there throughout. We started during the herbal era and evolved with each wave.

📝 EXAMPLE K-beauty has gone through several major waves. In the 1990s, traditional Korean herbal ingredients like ginseng and snail mucin inspired our early cosmetics. In the 2000s, BB cream became a global phenomenon. Around 2014, sheet masks and 10-step skincare routines went viral. Then K-pop and K-drama in the 2020s changed everything — fans wanted to look like their favorite idols, and K-beauty went global. Now Korea is one of the leading cosmetic exporters worldwide.
