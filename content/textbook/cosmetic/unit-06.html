2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
💪 Scene 1. Introducing the Joint Care Line (관절케어 라인 소개)
David
Next, I'd like to introduce another popular line — our joint care series.
Buyer
Joint care? That's interesting. Tell me more.
David
We offer two versions, hot and cool, for different needs.
Buyer
What's the difference between them?
David
The hot gel provides warmth for stiff joints, while the cool gel soothes sore muscles after activity.
Buyer
Smart. What are the main ingredients?
David
The hot version contains capsaicin and ginger extract. The cool version uses menthol and peppermint.
Buyer
All natural extracts. That's appealing for today's consumers.
📺 Scene 2. Media Exposure & Senior Market (방송 사례와 시니어 시장)
David
This product was featured on Korean national TV last year.
Buyer
Really? Which program?
David
A popular health show on Channel A. After that, our sales tripled within a month.
Buyer
Impressive. So the senior demographic responds well to this product?
David
Exactly. About 70% of our buyers are over fifty.
Buyer
Interesting. The senior market is growing fast in the U.S. too.
David
That's why I think this line has strong potential for your stores.
Buyer
I agree. Could you share clinical data and customer testimonials?
David
Absolutely. I'll send everything by email this afternoon.
3. ASK · 튜터와 이야기하기 7 MIN

📝 EXAMPLE Next, I'd like to introduce our joint care series. We offer two versions — hot and cool — for different needs. The hot gel contains capsaicin for stiff joints, while the cool gel uses menthol to soothe sore muscles. The product was featured on Channel A last year, and our sales tripled in a month. About 70% of our buyers are over fifty, so I believe it has strong potential for the U.S. senior market.
