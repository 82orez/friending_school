민성(David)은 (주)웰빙헬스팜의 해외마케팅부 부장이다. 오늘은 미국 보스턴에서 온 첫 바이어 Sarah Miller를 인천공항에서 픽업해 호텔까지 안내해야 한다. 첫인상이 거래의 시작을 좌우하기에, 자기소개와 일정 안내, 자연스러운 아이스브레이킹까지 모두 영어로 매끄럽게 진행해야 한다.

2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🛬 Scene 1. At Incheon Airport Arrival Hall (인천공항 입국장에서의 첫 만남)
David
Hi, are you Sarah from Boston Beauty? I'm David Park from Wellbeing Healthfarm.
Buyer
Yes, that's me. Nice to finally meet you in person, David.
David
The pleasure is mine. Welcome to Korea. How was your flight?
Buyer
Long, but smooth. I managed to get some sleep, so I feel okay.
David
Glad to hear that. Let me help you with your luggage. Our car is just outside.
Buyer
Thank you so much. I really appreciate you picking me up personally.
David
It's my pleasure. You're our first international guest of the year, so I wanted to make sure everything goes smoothly.
Buyer
That's very kind. I've been looking forward to this trip for months.
🚗 Scene 2. On the Way to the Hotel (호텔로 가는 차 안에서)
David
It takes about an hour to reach your hotel in Seoul. Let me walk you through tomorrow's schedule.
Buyer
Sure, please go ahead.
David
We'll pick you up at 9 a.m. and head to our headquarters in Incheon. You'll meet our team and tour the production facility.
Buyer
That sounds great. How long have you been with the company?
David
A few years now. I'll share more about our brand and products over dinner tomorrow.
Buyer
I'd love that. By the way, is there anything I should be careful about in business meetings here?
David
Just relax. We exchange business cards with both hands, but other than that, it's quite similar to the U.S.
Buyer
Good to know. I'm really looking forward to seeing your products tomorrow.
David
So am I. Here's my business card — please feel free to reach out anytime during your stay.
3. ASK · 튜터와 이야기하기 7 MIN
Q1
Have you ever picked up a foreign buyer at the airport? How did it go?
Q2
What's the most important thing when you meet a buyer for the first time?
Q3
How do you usually break the ice with someone from a different culture?
Q4
What do you think a foreign buyer wants to hear during a first meeting?
Q5
If your buyer arrived tired after a long flight, what would you do first?
MISSION
공항에서 첫 바이어를 픽업해 호텔까지 안내하기
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 바이어를 알아보고 영어로 자기소개 하기 (이름·직책·회사)
② 비행 안부 묻고 따뜻하게 환영하기
③ 호텔로 이동하며 내일 일정 자연스럽게 안내하기
④ 명함을 건네며 "언제든 연락 달라"고 정중히 마무리하기
📝 EXAMPLE Hi, are you Sarah from Boston Beauty? I'm David Park, in charge of overseas marketing at Wellbeing Healthfarm. Welcome to Korea — how was your flight? It takes about an hour to reach your hotel, so let me walk you through tomorrow's schedule on the way. Here's my business card. Please feel free to reach out anytime during your stay.
