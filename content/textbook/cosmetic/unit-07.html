2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
💆‍♀️ Scene 1. The Full Lineup (전체 라인업 소개)
David
Our facial skincare range, called Yeppen Eolgul, covers all major skin concerns.
Buyer
Yeppen Eolgul? What does that mean?
David
It means 'pretty face' in Korean. We have four key products in this line.
Buyer
Could you walk me through them?
David
Sure. The collagen cream targets anti-aging, vitamin C brightens, cica soothes, and we also have a daily sunscreen.
Buyer
A complete routine, then.
David
Exactly. Customers can build a full regimen using just our line.
Buyer
That's a strong selling point. Are these all in the same price tier?
💰 Scene 2. Pricing & Selection (가격대 비교 및 매장 도입 추천)
David
Most items retail between $18 and $35, making them accessible to a wide audience.
Buyer
Which item would you recommend starting with?
David
For first-time buyers, I'd suggest the cica cream. It's our top seller in the facial line.
Buyer
Why is cica so popular?
David
Sensitive skin is a global concern now, and cica works for almost everyone.
Buyer
Good logic. Could we start with cica and add other items later?
David
Absolutely. Many of our overseas partners start with one or two items and expand.
Buyer
Sounds like a sensible approach. Let's go with that.
