2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
☕ Scene 1. Background & Education (배경과 학업)
Buyer
Your English is excellent, David. Did you study abroad?
David
Thank you. Yes, I studied business administration at a university in California.
Buyer
Oh nice, which one?
David
UC San Diego. I was there for about four years.
Buyer
What was your favorite part of living in the U.S.?
David
The diversity, honestly. So many cultures in one place — it shaped how I think about business.
Buyer
And after graduation?
David
I worked at a marketing firm in San Francisco for two years before coming back to Korea.
🎯 Scene 2. Career Journey & Vision (커리어 여정과 비전)
Buyer
What brought you back?
David
I wanted to apply what I learned abroad to a Korean brand. The cosmetics industry was growing fast, and the timing felt right.
Buyer
Why this company specifically?
David
I respected their long-term focus on quality. Many cosmetics brands chase trends — this one builds for the long term.
Buyer
Makes sense. What's your goal here?
David
My goal is to grow this brand into a global name. Korea's domestic market is great, but I want our products in stores worldwide.
Buyer
Ambitious. I like it.
David
What about you? How did you end up in the beauty retail business?
Buyer
Long story — but I started by working at a department store right out of college, and I just fell in love with the industry.
David
That passion really shows in how you talk about your stores.
