2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🏢 Scene 1. Entering the Headquarters (본사 로비에서 회사 소개)
David
Welcome to our headquarters. Let me give you a quick tour of our facility.
Buyer
Thank you. It's bigger than I expected.
David
We've been in the cosmetics business for over two decades, specializing in foot care.
Buyer
That's impressive. So foot care is your main expertise?
David
Exactly. We're known as the leading foot cream brand in Korea.
Buyer
Interesting. I'd love to learn more about your manufacturing process.
David
Sure. Let's start with our R&D center on the second floor.
Buyer
Sounds great. Lead the way.
🔬 Scene 2. At the R&D Center (R&D 센터 견학)
David
This is our R&D center, where we develop all new formulas.
Buyer
How many researchers work here?
David
We have twelve full-time researchers, including three with PhDs in dermatology.
Buyer
That's a strong team for a mid-sized company.
David
Thank you. We also collaborate with university labs for advanced research.
Buyer
Do you handle stability and safety testing in-house?
David
Yes, all stability tests are done here. Safety tests are outsourced to certified labs.
Buyer
Good. Could we see the production line next?
David
Of course. Please follow me to the manufacturing floor.
