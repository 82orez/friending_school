2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
📧 Scene 1. Planning the Follow-up Email (후속 이메일 구상)
David
Sarah, before you leave, I'll send a follow-up email summarizing today's discussion.
Buyer
Thank you. I always appreciate a written record.
David
I'll include the trial order terms, marketing support package, and exclusivity framework.
Buyer
Perfect. When should I expect it?
David
Within two hours after you leave. I want everything fresh while we both remember the details.
Buyer
That's professional. What do you need from me?
David
Just a quick confirmation that my summary matches your understanding.
Buyer
Will do. I'll reply by tomorrow morning.
✍️ Scene 2. Email Structure Principles (이메일 구조 원칙)
Buyer
Out of curiosity, what's your email style? You seem to send a lot of detailed emails.
David
I follow a simple four-part structure. Subject line, greeting, body, and clear next steps.
Buyer
Tell me about the subject line.
David
I make it specific. Instead of 'Meeting Follow-up,' I'd write 'Follow-up: Trial Order Terms & Marketing Support Package.'
Buyer
Much clearer. What about the body?
David
Three short paragraphs at most. Summary, ask, and timeline.
Buyer
And the close?
David
Always with a clear next step. Not 'let me know if you have questions,' but something like 'I'll send the contract by Friday.'
Buyer
That's actionable. I should adopt that style myself.

MISSION
미팅 후속 이메일 4단계 구조로 작성하기
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 구체적인 Subject Line 만들기
② 미팅 감사 인사로 시작하기
③ 논의한 핵심 사항 요약하기
④ 다음 단계와 시점 명확하게 마무리하기
📝 EXAMPLE I follow a simple four-part structure. The subject line is specific — 'Follow-up: Trial Order Terms & Marketing Support Package' is much clearer than just 'Meeting Follow-up.' The body has three short paragraphs at most — summary, ask, and timeline. And I always close with a clear next step, not 'let me know if you have questions,' but something concrete like 'I'll send the contract by Friday.'
