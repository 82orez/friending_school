2
3WB is our premium line for high-end retailers.

Which line do you think fits your channel?
어느 라인이 귀사의 유통 채널에 맞을 것 같으세요?

2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🏷️ Scene 1. Explaining the Three Lines (3개 브랜드 라인 설명)
David
Today I'd like to introduce our three brand lines.
Buyer
Three lines? That's quite a portfolio.
David
Yes. Each line targets a different market segment and distribution channel.
Buyer
Could you walk me through them?
David
Sure. 3WB is our premium line, positioned for department stores and high-end retailers.
Buyer
What about WHB?
David
WHB is sold mainly through pharmacy channels. It focuses on functional, dermatologist-friendly products.
Buyer
And Wellbeing Healthfarm?
David
That's our mass-market line, sold at general retail stores and online platforms.
🎯 Scene 2. Choosing the Right Line (미국 시장에 맞는 라인 선정)
Buyer
For our boutique chain, which line would you recommend?
David
Based on what you've told me about your customers, I'd suggest 3WB.
Buyer
Why 3WB specifically?
David
Your stores attract premium customers, and 3WB's packaging and pricing match that segment.
Buyer
Makes sense. What's the price range for 3WB?
David
Retail prices range from $25 to $80, depending on the product.
Buyer
That works for us. Could you send detailed product specs?
David
Of course. I'll email you the 3WB catalog with full specifications today.
