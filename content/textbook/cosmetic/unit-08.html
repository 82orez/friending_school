2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
❓ Scene 1. Initial Questions (초기 질문 응대)
Buyer
Now that I've seen the products, I have a few questions.
David
Of course. Please ask anything you'd like.
Buyer
What's your minimum order quantity for the 3WB line?
David
Our standard MOQ is 500 units per SKU for the premium line.
Buyer
And what's your typical lead time?
David
Six to eight weeks from order confirmation, depending on the volume.
Buyer
Can we do a smaller trial order for the first shipment?
David
We can sometimes flex on MOQ for trial orders. Let me check with my team and get back to you.
🤝 Scene 2. Handling Difficult Questions (답하기 어려운 질문 응대)
Buyer
What about FDA registration? Are your products approved for the U.S.?
David
Most of our cosmetics line follows FDA cosmetic regulations. For specific items, let me confirm and send you the exact status.
Buyer
Sure. And do you offer marketing support, like product images and copy?
David
Yes, we provide high-resolution images and English product copy for all overseas partners.
Buyer
Good. What about exclusive distribution rights?
David
That's a great question, but it depends on volume commitments. I'd rather discuss it after our internal review.
Buyer
Fair enough. When can I expect a full answer?
David
I'll follow up with all the details by tomorrow morning. Is that timing okay?
Buyer
Perfect. Thank you for being so thorough.
3. ASK · 튜터와 이야기하기 7 MIN
Q1

Q2

Q3

Q4
How quickly should you follow up after a buyer meeting?
Q5

MISSION
바이어의 첫 질문에 정중하고 정확하게 응대하기
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 자신 있게 답할 수 있는 부분(MOQ, 납기) 답하기
② 트라이얼 오더 등 유연한 옵션 가능성 언급하기
③ 모르는 부분은 정중하게 "확인 후 답변" 약속하기
④ 명확한 후속 답변 시점 약속하기
📝 EXAMPLE Of course, please ask anything. Our standard MOQ is 500 units per SKU, and lead time is six to eight weeks. For a trial order, we can sometimes flex on the MOQ — let me check with my team. As for FDA registration and exclusive rights, I'd rather confirm the exact status than guess. I'll follow up with all the details by tomorrow morning.
