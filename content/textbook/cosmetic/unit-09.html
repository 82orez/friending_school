2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🥩 Scene 1. Arriving at the Restaurant (식당 도착과 자리 안내)
David
I thought we could try authentic Korean barbecue tonight.
Buyer
I've heard so much about it. I'm excited!
David
Great. This place specializes in samgyeopsal — grilled pork belly.
Buyer
Pork belly? Sounds delicious.
David
We grill the meat right at our table. It's interactive and fun.
Buyer
Oh, like a hibachi-style experience?
David
Similar idea, but we eat it differently. I'll show you in a moment.
Buyer
Perfect. Lead the way.
🥬 Scene 2. How to Eat It (쌈 싸 먹는 방법 설명)
David
Let me show you the Korean way. First, take a piece of lettuce.
Buyer
Like a wrap?
David
Exactly. Put a slice of grilled pork on it, then a bit of garlic and ssamjang — that's the dipping sauce.
Buyer
Got it. What about all these little dishes?
David
Those are banchan — Korean side dishes. They come free with the meal.
Buyer
Free? Wow. There are so many of them!
David
Yes, sharing is a big part of Korean dining culture. Try kimchi with the pork — they go well together.
Buyer
Mmm, this is amazing. The flavors balance perfectly.
David
Don't be shy — pile up the wrap and eat it in one bite. That's the traditional way.

📝 EXAMPLE I thought we could try authentic Korean barbecue tonight. This place specializes in samgyeopsal — grilled pork belly. We grill the meat right at our table. Let me show you how to eat it. Take a piece of lettuce, put a slice of pork on it, then add garlic and ssamjang. The little side dishes are called banchan, and they come free with the meal. Pile up your wrap and eat it in one bite — that's the traditional way.
