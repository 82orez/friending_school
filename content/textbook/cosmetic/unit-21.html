2. TALK · 실전 비즈니스 대화 9 MIN
🤝 Scene 1. Reviewing the Buyer's Email (바이어 메일 검토)
David
Hi Sarah, I got your email about the additional 5% discount request.
Buyer
Yes, my team really pushed for it after the sample test.
David
I appreciate the direct feedback. Could you share what's driving the request?
Buyer
Margin pressure. Our retail price has to compete with established brands.
David
That makes sense. I'd like to respond properly — let me reply by email so we have it documented.
Buyer
Sure, that works. When can I expect it?
David
By end of day. I'll propose an alternative that protects both our margins.
Buyer
I appreciate that approach. Let's see what you come up with.
✍️ Scene 2. Persuasive Email Structure (설득형 이메일 구조)
Buyer
What's your approach when writing negotiation emails?
David
I follow a three-part structure — acknowledge, explain, propose.
Buyer
Tell me more.
David
First, acknowledge their request without dismissing it. Then explain why a direct yes isn't feasible. Finally, propose a creative alternative.
Buyer
What kind of alternative usually works?
David
Performance-based incentives. A rebate after hitting targets, for example, instead of an upfront discount.
Buyer
Smart — it shifts the risk from you to shared outcomes.
David
Exactly. And I always close with a request for a call, not just a reply. Real negotiation happens in conversation.
Buyer
I look forward to seeing your proposal.
David
Same here. I'll have it to you by 6 p.m. Korea time.

협상 제안 이메일 3단계 구조로 작성하기
아래 네 가지 내용을 이어서 한 번에 자연스럽게 말해보는 것이 오늘의 목표입니다. · 6 MIN
① 바이어 요청에 대한 인정과 감사로 시작하기
② 직접 할인이 어려운 이유 정중히 설명하기
③ 성과 기반 리베이트 등 대안 제안하기
④ 추가 논의를 위한 통화 제안으로 마무리하기
📝 EXAMPLE I follow a three-part structure — acknowledge, explain, propose. First, I acknowledge the buyer's request without dismissing it. Then I explain why a direct yes isn't feasible. Finally, I propose a creative alternative — usually a performance-based rebate after hitting targets, instead of an upfront discount. This shifts the risk from us to shared outcomes. And I always close with a request for a call, not just a reply.
