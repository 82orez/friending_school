2. TALK · 실전 대화
📍 At the Telecom Store (통신사 매장에서)
Staff
Hi, how can I help you?
Jun
Hi, I need to buy a SIM card.
Staff
Sure. What kind of plan do you want?
Jun
I want a prepaid plan. How much data can I get?
Staff
For $30, you get 30GB and unlimited calls.
Jun
That sounds good. How long does it last?
Staff
It lasts for 28 days.
Jun
Okay, I'll take it.
