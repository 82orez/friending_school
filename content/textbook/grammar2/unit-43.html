UNIT 43

~한 것이에요!

관계대명사 which — 사물·동물을 설명할 때
POINT

사람 → who  |  사물·동물 → which. 두 문장에서 겹치는 사물·동물을 which로 연결해서 한 문장으로 만들어요!

명사(사물·동물) + which + 동사 예) a movie which was sad  ·  the phone which I bought
💡 3단계로 만들어요

① I bought a car. The car was imported from Germany. → 중복: car
② The car → which → which was imported from Germany
③ I bought a car which was imported from Germany.
나는 독일에서 수입된 차를 샀어요. (겹치는 명사를 which로 바꿔 연결!)

관계대명사받는 말예문
who
(사람)		He / SheI know a woman who speaks English.
which
(사물·동물)		ItI watched a movie which was sad.
아주 슬픈 영화를 봤어요.
I watched a movie which was very sad.
이게 어제 제가 산 핸드폰이에요.
This is the phone which I bought yesterday.
온라인에서 유명한 식당에 갔어요.
I visited a restaurant which was famous online.
많이 짖는 개를 키워요.
I have a dog which barks a lot.
📌 포인트: 사람 → who  |  사물·동물 → which  |  명사 + which + 동사 = "~한 것"  |  회화에서는 which 대신 that도 많이 써요.

📍 Scene. Talking about dramas
Anna: Are you watching any drama these days?
Ben: Yes! I'm watching a drama which is very popular right now.
Anna: What drama is it?
Ben: It's a drama which is famous online. The story is so touching!
Anna: What part do you like the most?
Ben: The acting is amazing. I cried so much watching it.
Anna: I should watch it too!
Ben: You should. You won't regret it!

a drama which is popular
