2. TALK · 실전 대화
📍 At a Café with Friends (친구들과 카페에서)
Marco
Hey Jun, what do you like to do in your free time?
Jun
I like watching movies. How about you?
Marco
I love cooking. I'm from Italy, so I make pasta a lot.
Jun
Cool! What did you do last weekend?
Marco
I went to the beach with friends. And you?
Jun
I just stayed home and watched Netflix.
Marco
Let's hang out together next weekend!
Jun
Sounds great. I'd love to.
3. EXTENSION · 응용 표현
1I like ~ing
I like watching movies.
I like playing games.
I like cooking food.
I like meeting new people.
2I'm from ~
I'm from Italy.
I'm from a small town.
I'm from the south of France.
I'm from near the beach.
3What did you do ~?
What did you do last weekend?
What did you do yesterday?
What did you do last night?
What did you do this morning?
