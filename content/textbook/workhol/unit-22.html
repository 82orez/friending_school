2. TALK · 실전 대화
📍 Scene 1 · Talking About Schedule (스케줄 이야기)
Jun
Can I see my schedule for next week?
Manager
Sure, you're working Tuesday to Saturday.
Jun
Can I have next Friday off? I have a doctor's appointment.
Manager
Let me check... yes, that's fine.
Jun
Thank you so much.
📍 Scene 2 · Asking for Feedback (피드백 요청)
Jun
How am I doing so far?
Manager
You're doing well. You work hard and listen carefully.
Jun
Is there anything to improve?
Manager
Try to be a bit faster during the rush.
Jun
Okay, I'll work on that. I appreciate the feedback.
