2. TALK · 실전 대화
📍 Talking About Career (경력 설명)
Chef
Tell me more about your cooking experience.
Jun
I worked in Korea for three years.
Chef
What kind of food did you make?
Jun
I make Korean and Western food.
Chef
Did you study cooking?
Jun
Yes, I studied at a cooking school for two years.
Chef
What dishes are you good at?
Jun
I'm good at pasta and meat dishes.
Chef
Great. We make Italian food here.
Jun
I want to learn more here.

2I can make ~
I can make Korean food.
I can make pasta.
I can make desserts.
I can make many dishes.
3I'm good at ~
I'm good at pasta.
I'm good at meat dishes.
I'm good at Korean BBQ.
I'm good at sauces.
