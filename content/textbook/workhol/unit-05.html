2. TALK · 실전 대화
📍 Scene 1 · Looking for an Item (물건 찾기)
Jun
Excuse me, where can I find ramen?
Staff
It's in aisle 7, with the Asian food.
Jun
Thank you. Do you have Korean rice?
Staff
Yes, it's on the same shelf.
📍 Scene 2 · At the Checkout (계산대에서)
Cashier
Hi, did you find everything?
Jun
Yes, thanks. How much is this?
Cashier
That's $4.50.
Jun
Okay, I'll take it. Can I pay by card?
Cashier
Sure. Tap or insert your card, please.
Jun
Here you go.
