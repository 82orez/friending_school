2. TALK · 실전 대화
📍 Scene 1 · Small Talk (스몰토크)
Sam
Hey Jun, how was your weekend?
Jun
It was good. I cooked some Korean food. How about you?
Sam
Just relaxed at home. Have you ever tried Vegemite?
Jun
Yes, once. It was... interesting!
Sam
Haha, that's funny! Most people hate it.
📍 Scene 2 · Talking About Korean Food (한국 음식 이야기)
Sam
What Korean food do you recommend?
Jun
You should try kimchi. It's spicy and sour.
Sam
Sounds good. What else?
Jun
Korean BBQ! It's really popular.
Sam
Let's eat together sometime.
Jun
Sure! I'll take you to a Korean restaurant.
