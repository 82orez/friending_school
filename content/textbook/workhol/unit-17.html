2. TALK · 실전 대화
📍 Scene 1 · FIFO Lifestyle Question (FIFO 생활 질문)
Manager
FIFO life is hard. Are you ready for it?
Jun
Yes, I'm ready for FIFO life.
Manager
You'll be away from home for weeks. Is that okay?
Jun
Yes, I don't mind being away from home.
Manager
How do you handle 12-hour shifts?
Jun
I rest before each shift. I sleep well.
📍 Scene 2 · Stress Management (스트레스 관리)
Manager
How do you manage stress?
Jun
I listen to music to relax. I also call my family.
Manager
Good. We need workers who are mentally strong.
Jun
I take my work seriously and stay positive.
Manager
Great attitude. We'll let you know.
