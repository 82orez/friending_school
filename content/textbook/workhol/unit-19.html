2. TALK · 실전 대화
📍 Scene 1 · Asking to Repeat (다시 말해달라고 하기)
Chef
Jun, chop onions, dice tomatoes, and prep the sauce!
Jun
Sorry, could you repeat that?
Chef
Chop onions, dice tomatoes, prep the sauce.
Jun
Can you speak a little slower?
Chef
Sure. Chop... onions. Dice... tomatoes. Prep... the sauce.
Jun
Got it. Thank you.
📍 Scene 2 · Confirming the Task (업무 확인)
Jun
Does that mean small pieces for the tomatoes?
Chef
Yes, very small. Like this.
Jun
Let me make sure I got it. Onions chopped, tomatoes diced small, then sauce?
Chef
Exactly.
Jun
Okay, I'll get on it.
3. EXTENSION · 응용 표현
1Could you ~?
Could you repeat that?
Could you say that again?
Could you write it down?
Could you show me?
2Can you speak ~?
Can you speak slower?
Can you speak louder?
Can you speak in simple English?
Can you speak one more time?
3Let me make sure ~
Let me make sure I got it.
Let me make sure I understand.
Let me make sure that's right.
Let me make sure I'm doing this right.
