PART 5 · SERVICE COMMUNICATION
UNIT 20
Rush Hour Communication
준, 러시 아워를 견뎌내다
⏱️

SITUATION

주방이 폭주(slammed, 폭주한)하는 러시 아워(rush, 폭주 시간)에는 짧고 빠른 영어가 필수다. 셰프는 모두에게 "We're slammed!"라고 외치며 분위기를 다잡는다. 러시에는 프렙(prep, 준비된 재료)이 빠르게 떨어진다 — 어떤 재료가 러닝 로(running low, 거의 떨어지는 상태)인지 즉시 보고해야 한다. 백업(backup, 예비 재료)을 미리 꺼내두는 것이 셰프의 생존 비결이다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
We're slammed tonight!
오늘 밤 폭주다!
2
We're running low on fries, Chef.
프라이가 거의 다 떨어졌어요, 셰프.
3
Big rush at seven sharp.
7시 정각에 큰 러시 시작이다.
4
Pull more prep from the walk-in.
워크인에서 프렙 더 꺼내라.
5
Get the backup ready, please.
백업 준비해 둬라.
▸ WORD BANK
slammed running low rush prep backup
2

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Before the Rush
러시 시작 전 브리핑
Chef
Jun, listen up. We're slammed tonight. Sixty covers in two hours.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Big rush?
Chef
Right. Pull all backup from the walk-in. Prep on standby.
Jun
Heard, Chef! Pull all backup, prep ready.
Chef
Speed is everything. Call out as soon as anything runs low.
Jun
Yes, Chef!
Scene 2 — Mid-Rush Status
러시 중 상황 보고
Sous Chef
Jun, status check. How's the line?
Jun
We're running low on fries, Chef. Two portions left.
Sous Chef
Drop a fresh batch. Now. What else is low?
Jun
Backup mash is also low. Almost out, Chef.
Sous Chef
I'll pull more prep. You handle the line.
Jun
Yes, Chef!
3

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · We're slammed / running low / out of ~
We're slammed tonight!
We're running low on fries.
We're out of mashed potatoes, Chef.
We're running low on sauce.
Pattern 2 · Big rush at ~
Big rush at seven sharp.
Big rush at eight, fifty covers.
Big rush at lunch tomorrow.
Rush starts in ten minutes!
Pattern 3 · Pull / Get the backup ~
Pull the backup from the walk-in.
Get the backup ready, please.
Pull more prep now.
Get the backup mash on standby.

MISSION · 오늘의 미션

🎯 오늘 셰프트에서 꼭 말해보기
러시 시작 시 "We're slammed tonight!" 한 번 발화하기.
재고 부족 시 "We're running low on fries." 표현 사용하기.
동료에게 "Get the backup ready." 한 번 지시해 보기.
slammed · rush · prep · running low · backup 중 3개 이상 발화하기.
▸ EXAMPLE
Yes, Chef! We're slammed tonight — sixty covers in two hours. I've pulled all backup from the walk-in and the prep is on standby. We're running low on fries — I'll drop a fresh batch now. Backup mash is also low. Big rush at seven sharp, Chef!
