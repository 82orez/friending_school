PART 3 · FLAVOR & TEXTURE
UNIT 09
Caramelizing & Sweating
준, 캐러멜라이징과 스웨팅을 익히다
🌿

SITUATION

준은 향채의 풍미를 끌어올리는 두 가지 핵심 기법을 배운다. 약한 불에서 뚜껑을 덮고 색은 내지 않고 부드럽게 익히는 스웨팅(sweat, 약불에 색 없이 익히기), 그리고 더 강한 불에서 노릇한 색과 단맛을 끌어내는 캐러멜라이징(caramelize, 노릇하게 단맛 끌어올리기)이다. 또한 고기 표면을 갈변시키는 브라우닝(brown, 갈변시키기), 채소를 부드럽게 익히는 소프트닝(soften, 부드럽게 익히기), 마지막에 윤기를 주는 글레이즈(glaze, 윤기내기)도 함께 익힌다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
Sweat the onions first, low heat.
먼저 양파를 약불로 스웨트해라.
2
Caramelize the sugar evenly.
설탕을 균일하게 캐러멜라이즈해라.
3
Brown the meat in the hot pan.
뜨거운 팬에 고기를 갈변시켜라.
4
Soften the carrots in butter.
버터에 당근을 부드럽게 익혀라.
5
Glaze the carrots with honey.
꿀로 당근에 윤기를 내라.
▸ WORD BANK
sweat caramelize brown soften glaze
2

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Building a Soup Base
수프 베이스 만들기
Chef
Jun, start the onion soup base. Low heat to begin.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Sweat the onions first — lid on, no color?
Chef
Right. Ten minutes. Then soften the carrots in the same pan.
Jun
Heard, Chef.
Chef
After that, brown the meat hard. We want deep color.
Jun
Yes, Chef!
Scene 2 — Dessert and Garnish
디저트와 가니쉬
Sous Chef
Jun, the crème brûlée needs sugar tops. Use the torch.
Jun
Yes, Chef!
Sous Chef
Caramelize the sugar evenly. Move the flame slowly.
Jun
Heard, Chef. Caramelize the sugar evenly — even golden color.
Sous Chef
Then glaze the side carrots with honey for the steak plate.
Jun
Yes, Chef!

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · Sweat / Caramelize the ~
Sweat the leeks gently.
Sweat the shallots, no color.
Caramelize the onions for the soup.
Caramelize the apples in butter.
Pattern 2 · Brown / Soften / Glaze the ~
Brown the meat hard.
Soften the vegetables slowly.
Glaze the carrots with butter and honey.
Brown the butter for the sauce.
Pattern 3 · low / medium / high heat
Sweat the onions on low heat.
Caramelize on medium heat, slowly.
Brown the meat on high heat.
Keep it on low heat, lid on.

▸ EXAMPLE
Yes, Chef! I'll sweat the onions first on low heat, lid on, no color — about ten minutes. Then I'll soften the carrots in the same pan. For the dessert, I'll caramelize the sugar evenly with the torch — slow, even, golden. And I'll glaze the side carrots with honey before plating, Chef.
