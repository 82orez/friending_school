PART 1 · PREP & MISE EN PLACE
UNIT 02
Chopping & Cutting
준, 기본 칼질을 배우다
🥬

SITUATION

준은 미장플라스를 마치고 본격적으로 재료 손질을 시작한다. 셰프는 깨끗한 커팅 보드(cutting board, 도마)를 쓰라고 강조하며, 양파는 곱게 다지고(chop, 잘게 썰기), 빵은 얇게 자르고(slice), 당근은 깍둑썰기(dice)로, 마늘은 더 잘게 다지는(mince) 법을 알려준다. 균일한 칼질이 좋은 요리의 첫걸음이다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
Chop the onions finely.
양파를 곱게 다져라.
2
Use a clean cutting board.
깨끗한 도마를 사용해라.
3
Slice the bread thinly.
빵을 얇게 잘라라.
4
Dice the carrots into small cubes.
당근을 작은 큐브로 깍둑썰기 해라.
5
Mince the garlic before cooking.
조리 전에 마늘을 잘게 다져라.
▸ WORD BANK
chop cutting board slice dice mince
2

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Knife Skills 101
칼질 기초 배우기
Chef
Jun, ready for some knife work?
Jun
Yes, Chef!
Chef
Good. Always use a clean cutting board. Never cross-contaminate.
Jun
Use a clean cutting board. Got it, Chef. New board for each ingredient?
Chef
Right. Now, chop the onions finely. For the soup base.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Chop the onions finely.
Scene 2 — Many Cuts
다양한 썰기
Sous Chef
Jun, slice the bread thinly for the toast.
Jun
How thin, Chef?
Sous Chef
Half a centimeter. Even slices, please.
Jun
Heard, Chef.
Sous Chef
Then dice the carrots small. And mince the garlic finely.
Jun
Yes, Chef!

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · Chop / Slice / Dice / Mince the ~
Chop the parsley finely.
Slice the tomatoes evenly.
Dice the potatoes for the stew.
Mince the ginger small.
Pattern 2 · Use a clean ~
Use a clean cutting board.
Use a clean knife for the fish.
Use a clean bowl for the eggs.
Use a clean towel, please.
Pattern 3 · ~ it [finely / thinly / evenly]
Chop it finely.
Slice it thinly.
Cut it evenly.
Mince it very fine, Chef.

MISSION · 오늘의 미션

🎯 오늘 셰프트에서 꼭 말해보기
칼질 시작 전 "Use a clean cutting board." 영어로 한 번 말해 보기.
셰프가 "Chop the onions finely." 라고 하면 즉시 "Yes, Chef!"로 응답하기.
동료에게 "Slice it thinly." / "Dice it small." 등 패턴 한 번 사용하기.
chop · slice · dice · mince 중 3개 이상을 실제 발화로 써 보기.
▸ EXAMPLE
Yes, Chef! I'll use a clean cutting board. Let me chop the onions finely first, and then slice the bread thinly for the toast. After that, I'll dice the carrots into small cubes and mince the garlic. I'll keep all the cuts even, Chef.
