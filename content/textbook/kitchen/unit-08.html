PART 2 · BASIC COOKING METHODS
UNIT 08
Steaming & Roasting
준, 찜과 로스팅을 익히다
🔥

SITUATION

준은 오븐과 찜기를 사용하는 법을 배운다. 채소와 고기를 트레이(tray, 오븐 팬)에 올려 오븐랙(oven rack, 오븐 선반)에 넣고 로스트(roast, 오븐에 굽기)하면 표면이 캐러멜라이즈되며 풍미가 진해진다. 한편 만두나 생선은 찜기에 넣고 스팀(steam, 증기로 익히기)으로 부드럽게 익힌다. 빵과 케이크는 베이크(bake, 오븐에 굽기)한다. 같은 오븐도 용도에 따라 다른 표현을 쓴다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
Roast the vegetables at 200 degrees.
200도에서 채소를 로스트해라.
2
Steam the dumplings for eight minutes.
만두를 8분간 찐다.
3
Bake the bread until golden.
빵이 노릇해질 때까지 구워라.
4
Put them on a clean tray.
깨끗한 트레이에 올려라.
5
Move it to the top oven rack.
오븐 윗단으로 옮겨라.
▸ WORD BANK
roast steam bake tray oven rack
2

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Roasting Vegetables
채소 로스팅
Chef
Jun, prep the side. Roast the vegetables for dinner service.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Roast the vegetables — what temperature?
Chef
200 degrees Celsius. Oil and salt them first. Toss well.
Jun
Heard, Chef. Big tray, single layer?
Chef
Right. Not too crowded. Middle rack for thirty minutes.
Jun
Yes, Chef!
Scene 2 — Steamer Station
찜기 스테이션에서
Sous Chef
Jun, six dumplings for table two. On the fly.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Steam the dumplings — eight minutes, bamboo basket?
Sous Chef
Right. High steam. And check the bread in the oven.
Jun
Heard, Chef.
Sous Chef
Move the tray to the top oven rack to get color on top.
Jun
Yes, Chef!

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · Roast / Steam / Bake the ~
Roast the chicken at 180.
Steam the fish for six minutes.
Bake the pie until the crust is golden.
Roast the bones for the stock.
Pattern 2 · Put it on the ~
Put it on the tray.
Put it on the middle rack.
Put them on the bottom oven rack.
Put it on the pass for service.
Pattern 3 · Move it to ~
Move it to the top rack.
Move it to the cooler oven.
Move it to the side, Chef.
Move it to the pass.

MISSION · 오늘의 미션

🎯 오늘 셰프트에서 꼭 말해보기
로스팅 지시받으면 "Roast the vegetables." 한 번 복창하기.
찜기 사용 시 "Steam the dumplings." 큰 소리로 발화하기.
동료에게 "Put it on the tray." 또는 "Move it to the top rack." 사용하기.
roast · steam · bake · tray · oven rack 중 3개 이상 발화하기.
▸ EXAMPLE
Yes, Chef! I'll roast the vegetables at 200 degrees. First I oil and salt them, then put them on a big tray, single layer. Middle rack for thirty minutes. For the dumplings, I'll steam them in the bamboo basket for eight minutes. And I'll move the bread tray to the top oven rack for color, Chef.
