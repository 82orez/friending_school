PART 3 · FLAVOR & TEXTURE
UNIT 12
Thickening Techniques
준, 농도 조절 기법을 익히다
🌿

SITUATION

준은 소스의 농도를 조절하는 법을 배운다. 가장 클래식한 방법은 버터와 밀가루를 함께 볶아 만든 루(roux, 버터·밀가루 농후제)다. 더 빠르게는 옥수수전분(cornstarch, 옥수수전분)을 찬물에 풀어 만든 슬러리(slurry, 전분 물)를 끓는 소스에 풀어 넣는다. 졸여서(reduce, 졸이기) 수분을 자연스럽게 날리는 방법도 있다. 마지막으로 노른자와 크림으로 만든 리에종(liaison, 노른자·크림 농후제)은 부드럽고 우아한 마무리에 쓰인다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
Thicken it with roux, Chef.
루로 농도를 잡겠습니다, 셰프.
2
Make a slurry with water and starch.
물과 전분으로 슬러리를 만들어라.
3
Mix the cornstarch with cold water first.
옥수수전분을 먼저 찬물에 풀어라.
4
Let it reduce to thicken naturally.
졸여서 자연스럽게 농도를 잡아라.
5
Finish with a liaison for richness.
진한 풍미를 위해 리에종으로 마무리해라.
▸ WORD BANK
roux slurry cornstarch reduce liaison
2

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Béchamel with Roux
루로 베샤멜 만들기
Chef
Jun, we need béchamel for the gratin. Make it now.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Thicken it with roux — equal butter and flour?
Chef
Right. Cook the flour until pale gold. No raw taste.
Jun
Heard, Chef. Then whisk in warm milk slowly?
Chef
Yes, slowly. Whisk constantly so no lumps. Season with nutmeg.
Jun
Yes, Chef!
Scene 2 — Sauce on the Pass
패스에서 소스 마무리
Sous Chef
Jun, how's the demi-glace? Service in five.
Jun
Reducing now, Chef. Still a bit thin.
Sous Chef
Looking good — the sauce is reducing nicely. Give it two more minutes.
Jun
Heard, Chef.
Sous Chef
If it's still loose, make a slurry. Cornstarch and cold water.
Jun
Yes, Chef!

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · Thicken it with ~
Thicken it with roux.
Thicken it with a cornstarch slurry.
Thicken it with butter at the end.
Thicken the sauce with a liaison.
Pattern 2 · Make a slurry with ~
Make a slurry with cornstarch and water.
Make a slurry with flour and stock.
Make a slurry with potato starch, Chef.
Make a slurry, no lumps.
Pattern 3 · Let it ~ (reduce / thicken)
Let it reduce by half.
Let it thicken slowly.
Let it reduce until syrupy.
Let the sauce thicken on low heat.

MISSION · 오늘의 미션

🎯 오늘 셰프트에서 꼭 말해보기
소스 농도 조절 시 "Thicken it with roux." 한 번 발화하기.
졸이는 중 "The sauce is reducing nicely." 표현 사용하기.
동료에게 "Make a slurry with cornstarch." 한 번 지시해 보기.
roux · slurry · cornstarch · reduce · liaison 중 3개 이상 발화하기.
▸ EXAMPLE
Yes, Chef! For the béchamel, I'll thicken it with roux — equal butter and flour, cooked until pale gold, then warm milk whisked in slowly. The demi-glace is reducing nicely on the back burner. If it stays loose, I'll make a slurry with cornstarch and cold water. And the cream sauce will finish with a liaison, Chef.
