PART 3 · FLAVOR & TEXTURE
UNIT 10
Seasoning & Garnishing
준, 시즈닝과 가니쉬를 익히다
🌿

SITUATION

준은 요리의 마무리 단계를 배운다. 모든 요리는 시즈닝(season, 간하기)으로 맛이 완성된다. 셰프는 "맛보며 간하기"의 원칙을 강조한다. 향을 더하는 허브(herbs, 허브)를 잘게 다지고, 갓 갈은 크랙드 페퍼(cracked pepper, 굵게 간 후추)를 뿌린다. 마지막으로 플레이팅(plating, 접시 담기) 위에 가니쉬(garnish, 장식 재료)를 올려야 한 접시가 완성된다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
Season to taste, Chef.
맛보면서 간을 맞춰라, 셰프.
2
Garnish before serving.
서빙 전에 가니쉬를 올려라.
3
Add some fresh herbs on top.
위에 신선한 허브를 좀 올려라.
4
Finish with cracked pepper.
마무리로 굵게 간 후추를 뿌려라.
5
Check the plating before sending it out.
내보내기 전에 플레이팅을 확인해라.
▸ WORD BANK
season garnish herbs cracked pepper plating
2

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Tasting the Soup
수프 간 맞추기
Chef
Jun, taste the soup. How is it?
Jun
A bit flat, Chef. Needs more salt.
Chef
Right. Salt, pepper, maybe a touch of lemon.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Season to taste.
Chef
Always taste before sending it out. Never assume.
Jun
Heard, Chef!
Scene 2 — On the Pass
패스에서 마무리
Sous Chef
Jun, two plates ready for table six. Garnish before serving.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Garnish before serving — fresh herbs and cracked pepper?
Sous Chef
Right. Light on the herbs, generous on the pepper.
Jun
Got it, Chef.
Sous Chef
Wipe the rim. Plating must be clean before it leaves the pass.
Jun
Yes, Chef!

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · Season ~ (to taste / with ~)
Season the sauce to taste.
Season the meat with salt and pepper.
Season heavily, the pasta water needs salt.
Season just before cooking, Chef.
Pattern 2 · Garnish with ~
Garnish with fresh herbs.
Garnish with a lemon wedge.
Garnish with microgreens on top.
Garnish with a drizzle of olive oil.
Pattern 3 · Finish with ~
Finish with cracked pepper.
Finish with a pinch of sea salt.
Finish with olive oil and lemon.
Finish with chopped parsley, Chef.
