UNIT 11
Pureeing & Pulsing
준, 퓌레와 펄싱을 익히다
🌿

SITUATION

준은 부드러운 텍스처를 만드는 법을 배운다. 수프나 소스를 매끄럽게 만들려면 푸드 프로세서(food processor, 분쇄기) 또는 핸드블렌더에 재료를 넣고 퓌레(puree, 곱게 갈기)한다. 살사처럼 거친 식감을 원하면 잠깐씩 끊어 돌리는 펄스(pulse, 짧게 끊어 갈기)를 한다. 두 기법 모두 끝나면 매끄러운 텍스처(smooth texture, 부드러운 질감)와 균일한 블렌딩(blend, 골고루 섞기)이 핵심이다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
Puree the soup until smooth.
수프를 매끄러워질 때까지 갈아라.
2
Pulse it a few times only.
몇 번만 짧게 끊어서 돌려라.
3
Blend everything together.
전부 골고루 섞어라.
4
Use the big food processor.
큰 푸드 프로세서를 써라.
5
We need a smooth texture, Chef.
매끄러운 질감이 필요합니다, 셰프.

puree pulse blend food processor smooth texture

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Pumpkin Soup
호박 수프 만들기
Chef
Jun, the pumpkin is soft. Time to finish the soup.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Puree the soup — until smooth?
Chef
Right. Hand blender directly in the pot. Mind the splash.
Jun
Heard, Chef.
Chef
We need a smooth texture — no chunks at all.
Jun
Yes, Chef!
Scene 2 — Salsa Verde
살사 베르데 만들기
Sous Chef
Jun, salsa for the tacos. Use the food processor.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Puree it smooth?
Sous Chef
No, no. Don't puree it. Pulse it a few times only.
Jun
Heard, Chef. Pulse it a few times — chunky texture.
Sous Chef
Right. Then blend in lime juice and salt by hand.
Jun
Yes, Chef!

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · Puree / Pulse / Blend the ~
Puree the cauliflower.
Pulse the herbs lightly.
Blend the dressing until creamy.
Puree the beans for the dip.
Pattern 2 · Use the ~
Use the food processor.
Use the hand blender, less mess.
Use the small blender for the herbs.
Use the sieve after pureeing.
Pattern 3 · Until ~
Puree it until smooth.
Blend it until creamy.
Pulse it until just combined.
Process it until the texture is right.

▸ EXAMPLE
Yes, Chef! For the pumpkin soup, I'll puree it until smooth — hand blender directly in the pot, no chunks. We need a smooth texture. For the salsa, I'll use the food processor and pulse it a few times only — chunky texture. Then I'll blend in lime juice and salt by hand, Chef.
