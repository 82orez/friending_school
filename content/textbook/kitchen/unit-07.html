PART 2 · BASIC COOKING METHODS
UNIT 07
Poaching & Blanching
준, 포칭과 블랜칭을 익히다
🔥

SITUATION

준은 부드러운 익힘 기법을 배운다. 끓기 직전의 잔잔한 물에서 익히는 포칭(poach, 약한 물에서 천천히 익히기)은 계란이나 생선에 적합하다. 시금치나 콩 같은 채소는 끓는 소금물에 잠깐 데친(blanch, 데치기) 뒤, 얼음물(ice bath, 얼음물)에 충격(shock, 찬물로 즉시 식혀 조리 중단)을 줘 색을 살린다. 마지막으로 잘 빼서(drain, 물기 빼기) 사용한다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
Blanch the spinach for ten seconds.
시금치를 10초 동안 데쳐라.
2
Shock it in an ice bath right after.
바로 얼음물에 넣어 충격을 줘라.
3
Poach the eggs in gentle water.
잔잔한 물에서 계란을 포칭해라.
4
Prepare a large ice bath first.
먼저 큰 얼음물 통을 준비해라.
5
Drain the pasta well before plating.
플레이팅 전에 파스타 물기를 잘 빼라.
▸ WORD BANK
blanch shock poach ice bath drain
2

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Blanching Spinach
시금치 데치기
Chef
Jun, we need blanched spinach for the side dish.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Blanch the spinach — how long?
Chef
Ten seconds. Salt the water heavily, like the sea.
Jun
Heard, Chef!
Chef
Then shock it in an ice bath. Keep the color bright green.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Shock it in an ice bath.
Scene 2 — Poaching Eggs
계란 포칭하기
Sous Chef
Jun, two poached eggs for table four.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Gentle simmer with a little vinegar?
Sous Chef
Right. Three minutes for soft yolks. Then drain them on paper towel.
Jun
Heard, Chef.
Sous Chef
Trim the rough edges before plating.
Jun
Yes, Chef!

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · Blanch / Poach the ~
Blanch the green beans for thirty seconds.
Blanch the asparagus quickly.
Poach the salmon in court bouillon.
Poach the pears in red wine.
Pattern 2 · Shock it in ~
Shock it in an ice bath.
Shock it in cold water right away.
Shock the broccoli in ice water.
Shock them in ice to keep the color.
Pattern 3 · Drain it ~
Drain it well.
Drain it on paper towel.
Drain the pasta quickly.
Drain them in the colander, Chef.

MISSION · 오늘의 미션

🎯 오늘 셰프트에서 꼭 말해보기
채소 데치기 지시 받으면 "Blanch the spinach." 즉시 복창하기.
색을 살리기 위해 "Shock it in an ice bath." 표현 발화하기.
동료에게 "Drain it well, please." 한 번 지시하기.
blanch · poach · shock · drain · ice bath 중 3개 이상 발화하기.
▸ EXAMPLE
Yes, Chef! I'll blanch the spinach for ten seconds in salted water. Right after, I'll shock it in an ice bath to keep the color bright. After draining well, the spinach is ready for the side dish. For the eggs, I'll poach them gently for three minutes with a little vinegar, Chef.
