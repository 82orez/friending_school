PART 4 · CLASSIC FRENCH TECHNIQUES
UNIT 14
Confit & Slow Cooking
준, 콩피와 슬로우 쿠킹을 익히다
🍖

SITUATION

준은 프렌치 클래식 중에서도 가장 인내심을 요하는 기법인 콩피(confit, 지방에 잠겨 천천히 익히기)를 배운다. 오리 다리를 소금에 절인 뒤, 덕 팻(duck fat, 오리 기름)에 잠기게 해 약불(low heat, 약불)에서 2~3시간 천천히 익히면 텐더(tender, 부드러운)한 식감이 된다. 원래는 식재료를 보존(preserve, 보존하기)하기 위한 기법이었지만, 지금은 풍미를 위해 쓴다.

1

5 PICK · 핵심 문장 채우기

1
Cook it confit style.
콩피 방식으로 익혀라.
2
Use plenty of duck fat in the pot.
팟에 오리 기름을 넉넉히 넣어라.
3
Keep it on low heat for three hours.
3시간 동안 약불을 유지해라.
4
The meat should be very tender.
고기가 아주 부드러워야 한다.
5
This method helps preserve the meat.
이 방식은 고기를 보존하는 데도 좋다.
▸ WORD BANK
confit duck fat low heat tender preserve
2

TALK · 실전 대화

Scene 1 — Starting Duck Confit
오리 콩피 시작하기
Chef
Jun, the duck legs were salted overnight. Time for the confit.
Jun
Yes, Chef! Wipe them dry first?
Chef
Right. Then submerge them in duck fat. Cook it confit style.
Jun
Heard, Chef! Cook it confit style — fully covered?
Chef
Yes. Let it cook slowly on low heat. Two to three hours.
Jun
Yes, Chef!
Scene 2 — Checking Tenderness
부드러움 확인하기
Sous Chef
Jun, check the confit. How's the texture?
Jun
Very tender, Chef. The meat falls off the bone.
Sous Chef
Perfect. Cool it down in the fat — it will preserve for weeks.
Jun
Heard, Chef. Cool it in the duck fat first?
Sous Chef
Right. Store it submerged. For service, crisp the skin in a hot pan.
Jun
Yes, Chef!

EXTENSION · 응용 표현

Pattern 1 · Cook it ~ style
Cook it confit style.
Cook it braise style.
Cook it French style, Chef.
Cook it low and slow style.
Pattern 2 · Let it cook slowly ~
Let it cook slowly for three hours.
Let it cook slowly on low heat.
Let it cook slowly, no rush.
Let it cook slowly until tender.
Pattern 3 · It should be ~
It should be very tender.
It should be falling off the bone.
It should be soft, not mushy.
It should be done in two hours, Chef.

MISSION · 오늘의 미션

🎯 오늘 셰프트에서 꼭 말해보기
콩피 시작 시 "Cook it confit style." 한 번 발화하기.
약불 조절 시 "Let it cook slowly." 표현 사용하기.
동료에게 "Use plenty of duck fat." 한 번 지시해 보기.
confit · duck fat · low heat · tender · preserve 중 3개 이상 발화하기.
▸ EXAMPLE
Yes, Chef! The duck legs were salted overnight, so I'll wipe them dry and cook it confit style — fully submerged in duck fat. I'll let it cook slowly on low heat for two to three hours, until the meat is very tender. After that, I'll cool the legs in the fat — it preserves them for weeks, Chef.
